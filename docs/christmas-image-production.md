# Christmas image production

Generated with the built-in image tool on 18 October 2026. The existing Halloween character images were edit references for the **same** seven identities, rather than a replacement character cast. The original `public/logo.png` was the logo edit reference. Scenes were new generations in the same evergreen/gold festive direction.

All seven character masters and the logo were requested with genuine transparency and their delivered WebPs have verified alpha channels. Full silhouettes, faces and accessories were visually inspected. Characters retain their original stable filenames/IDs so shared placement controls remain compatible; Christmas art lives under a separate directory. Existing Halloween files and the original logo were not overwritten.

Total delivery size: 1,199,516 bytes. PNG masters are preserved in `docs/seasonal-asset-sources/christmas/` with corresponding base names. Exact per-image prompts and generation source filenames are recorded in `generation-prompts.json` there.

Character delivery preparation trimmed transparent margins, fitted each master into a 620 × 565 transparent area and added a ten-pixel transparent border, producing consistent 640 × 585 assets. WebP quality 82 / alpha quality 95 was used. Scenes were resized/cropped to the delivered dimensions at quality 82. The logo was trimmed and resized to 800px width at quality 85 / alpha quality 95. The footer preserves a 3:1 scene rather than the plan's proposed 4:1 crop to retain its books and low village skyline.

Ghost, owl, kitten, bat and fox wear Santa hats/scarves; the familiar pumpkin-headed robot has Christmas clothing and a present; bunny has a sweater and scarf with its mummy costume removed. These are new generated seasonal adaptations, not simple colour filters. No separate raster snow/sleigh animation assets are required: those effects use lightweight original CSS/vector markup.

No third-party stock recording or paid media was purchased. Optional ambience reuses the existing original Web Audio sound or an administrator's owned/licensed upload.
