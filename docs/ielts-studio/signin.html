How your work is marked

Practise against the same 9-band scale the test uses.

8

Very good userHandles complex language well, with only occasional slips.

7

Good userCopes with detailed reasoning; some mistakes in unfamiliar situations.

6

Competent userGenerally effective, with some inaccuracies and misunderstandings.

5

Modest userGets the overall meaning in most situations, with frequent errors.

Full mocks give a band for Listening, Reading and Writing, and an overall average of the three.
