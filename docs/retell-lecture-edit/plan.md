**Source:** `CapCut\Videos\1106 (2).mp4` is 12:21 long, 2560×1440, 30 fps. It's one fixed camera on the lecturer at the whiteboard, with Sinhala speech.

**Script:** `1106 (2)_cinescribe_project (1).json`. It has 183 lines, with the Sinhala original and an English translation.

**Goal:** turn a raw classroom recording into a polished SmartLabs YouTube lesson. It should look professionally shot and edited, with clean sound, an on-screen graphic for every key idea, full captions and a branded open and close. Her teaching stays the star, so nothing should cover her or the board she's pointing at.

## 2. The edit, top to bottom

### 2.1 Structure and length
```
0:00  Branded 3D intro (6 s)          "PTE Academic · Re-tell Lecture · Full strategy class"
0:06  Lesson starts at "Re-tell Lecture question type…" (raw 0:03.7; "Shall we start?" is trimmed)
      Chapter 1  What Re-tell Lecture tests        (raw 0:14)
      Chapter 2  How the question works (10 s / 40 s)  (raw 1:16)
      Chapter 3  How it's marked                    (raw 2:39)
      Chapter 4  Building a flow: Topic → Facts → Conclusion (raw 2:56)
      Chapter 5  The template that breaks 79        (raw 6:01)
      Chapter 6  The mistake that caps you at 50    (raw 7:13)
      Chapter 7  Fluency and pronunciation          (raw 10:12)
      Chapter 8  Why students stop at 65 or 75      (raw 11:54)
~11:50 Recap checklist and SmartLabs end card (12 s)
```
The finished video will be about 11:40–11:55. The chapter list will go into the YouTube description so viewers can jump to a section.

### 2.2 Clean-up (invisible editing)
- **Trim** "Shall we start?" at the head and the dead air at the tail.
- **Retakes:** keep only the clean take of each repeated line, using the times in the table above.
- **Pauses:** shorten pauses over 0.8 s down to about 0.35 s. Never cut mid-word, and only cut on a sentence boundary from the script.
- **Hiding the cuts:** every cut is also a camera change (see 2.3), so it reads as a two-camera shoot rather than a jump cut.

### 2.3 "Two-camera" look from one camera
The 1440p frame gives us three framings:

| Shot | Framing | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Wide** | Full frame: her and the whole board | While she writes or points at the board |
| **Medium** | 1.25× crop centred on her | Explaining to the class, every 2–4 sentences |
| **Board** | 1.3× crop on the part of the board she's writing on | "10 Sec", the Content/Fluency/Pronunciation circle, the template lines |

- **Keeping her in frame:** her bright shirt makes her easy to track automatically. Every frame I measure where she is, and the medium crop follows her smoothly, so she never drifts out of frame.
- **Push-ins:** 3–4 slow ones (100% to 108% over about 8 s) on the most important lines: "you cannot go beyond 50", "fluency is not speed", "79".
- **Punch-ins:** a sharp punch-in on emphasis words, used sparingly, about once a minute.

### 2.4 Graphics (the main upgrade)
These use the same SmartLabs PTE look as the "What is PTE" video: blue `#1A3FC4`, deep `#0F2A8A`, red `#E5343A`, yellow `#F5C518`, Space Grotesk and Plus Jakarta Sans fonts, 3D elements and motion blur.

**Placement:** when a graphic comes in, the video window slides aside and shrinks to about 68%, and the card fills the space it opens up. So nothing covers her face or the board. Short labels sit on the empty side of the frame, using the position tracking.

### 2.5 Captions
- **Source:** your script, cleaned. I'll fill the 4 empty lines, merge the duplicate and overlapping lines, and re-time every line to the audio.
- **Style:** short (2 lines max), on the PTE-style card, with keywords in blue. They move up automatically when a graphic needs the bottom of the frame.
- **Translation fixes**, for example "if you've killed mosquitoes really well" becomes "if you're unlucky", which is what the Sinhala idiom means.
- **Files for YouTube:** an English and a Sinhala `.srt`, so YouTube's CC button and search work too.

### 2.6 Sound
1. A light rumble filter, plus room-noise reduction (gentle, so her voice doesn't sound robotic).
2. Voice EQ for clarity, a soft compressor for an even level, and de-essing.
3. Loudness to **−14 LUFS** with a −1 dB peak ceiling, the YouTube standard.
4. **Music only** on the intro, the end card and very quietly under chapter cards. There's no music under her teaching, because research on learning videos shows background music under speech lowers recall.
5. Soft whoosh and pop effects on graphics, kept quiet.

### 2.7 Look
- A gentle grade: the wall and board a clean neutral white (they're slightly blue now), a touch of contrast, natural skin.
- Light sharpening on the zoomed shots so they match the wide.

## 3. How it gets built (technical)

1. **Clean script:** use Gemini to re-time the lines (the same method as the night class video), and fix the empty and duplicate lines. Output `script-clean.json`.
2. **Tracking:** sample the video at 5 fps and find her bright shirt to get a smooth left/right position for every frame.
3. **Edit list:** a timeline file with every cut, framing, zoom, chapter and graphic. The whole edit lives in this one file, so changes are quick.
4. **Base video (ffmpeg):** cuts, framings, zooms, grade and cleaned sound, kept lossless.
5. **Graphics (our 3D engine):** rendered only where they appear, about 4 minutes in total, with transparency and motion blur.
6. **Final:** layer graphics and captions over the base, then encode to H.264 1440p at high quality.
7. **Checks:** a still from every cut and graphic in a contact sheet. Confirm the frame count matches the edit list (the check that caught the earlier audio drift), and confirm captions match the audio.

**Time on this PC (i7-6700):** about 2–3 hours of building, then about 2½–3 hours of rendering. I'll send stills of the graphics first, so you approve the look before the long render.
